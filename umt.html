About the University

Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university is known for its focus on marine science, oceanography, fisheries and the sustainable use of natural resources.

UMT provides opportunities for students to develop their knowledge and skills through teaching, research and practical learning. Its academic areas also cover technology, food science, business, social sciences and other fields that support national development.

With its coastal location, UMT has a strong connection with the marine environment. The university encourages research, innovation and responsible practices to help address challenges involving the ocean and the community.
